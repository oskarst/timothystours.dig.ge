**Copy:** I don't use preset itineraries or sanitized routes. Every trip starts with a conversation to figure out exactly what kind of raw experience you're looking for. 

**Packages:**
*   **Tbilisi Day Tours ($180 / day):** Guided day tours in and around the capital.
*   **The Full Adventure - Base Rate ($240 / day):** Private guide, driver, and translator. We'll hit the dirt roads together, exploring the unseen sights and rugged character of Georgia.
*   **Mountain & Off-Road ($300 / day):** Specialized trips to mountain regions (Kazbegi, Svaneti, Khevsureti, Tusheti, Upper Adjara, Gudamakari, Vashlovani) or extreme off-road routes.

*These rates apply for groups of up to four adults (size of the Highlander – can include two children as well). Larger groups are of course welcome, but require hiring a larger vehicle; contact me for a quote applicable to your specific needs. On trips with overnight stays outside of Tbilisi, I ask guests to cover my overnight expenses (either at the same accommodation or with a per diem for my own accommodation). Rates exclude third-party activity fees, meals, entry tickets, and planning fees. If you are traveling in low season (November-April), I may be able to offer a discount – contact me for a specific quote.*
