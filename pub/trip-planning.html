Sample Inspiration

Every trip I lead is completely customizable. These are just a few examples of the off-road adventures we can build together.

Daytrip

The Khovle-Saorbisi Plateau

We head into a lost paradise high above the Tedzami Valley in Kartli. We start at Khovle to see a unique arched-brick aqueduct, then hit the jeep trail, ascending through the forest to a massive, grassy plateau. We'll walk up the large hill above Saorbisi to a spectacular 12th-century church with one of the best views in all of Georgia.

Daytrip

The Armenian Heritage of Lore

Bring your passports, because we're heading to the border zone. We'll explore the ruined cathedrals of Khorakert, Khojorni, and Brdadzori just meters from the frontier. Untouched by modern restoration, these ruins carry the heavy sensation of past glory from an era when the Lore region was a marchland of intermixed Georgian and Armenian culture.

Multi-Day Trek

Javakheti & Meskheti

We'll spend a few days exploring these distant regions. Depending on your stamina, we can climb to the incredibly well-preserved megalithic fortresses of Shaori or Abuli built on mountaintops thousands of years ago, or take it easy and watch migratory birds by the lakes. We'll crawl across the plateau backroads to the lip of the Mtkvari canyon for a rare, top-down view of the Vardzia Monastery, stay in village guesthouses, and slowly dawdle our way back through the mystical Uraveli valley.
