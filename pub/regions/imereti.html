Imereti

Subterranean Waterways

Imereti is a sprawling, decadent land that encompasses stark canyons and fertile plains. Do not settle for the standard day trips that trap travelers in a sanitized bubble. Venturing off the beaten track reveals the true character of this area, from the subterranean rivers of its limestone caves to the spectacular mountain panoramas of the Lesser Caucasus.

Colchian Gastronomy

The locals here possess a goofy humor and an intense dedication to indulgent gastronomy. While the tourist masses eat generic hotel meals, we pull up chairs to tables piled high with feasts of spiced bean soup and fresh cheese pastries. This is authentic, unpretentious hospitality where the food is deeply rooted in the agricultural reality of the Colchian plain.

Tsutskhvati Caverns & Zekari

The terrain demands to be explored with muddy boots and a healthy sense of curiosity. Hiking through the mystical Rioni valley reveals natural caverns at Tsutskhvati and hidden waterfalls in the Khani valley. Whether driving the winding plateau around Chiatura or climbing to the roof of the region at Zekari pass, the unfettered beauty of the land is undeniable.

Embrace The Grit

I know the hidden secrets of Imereti because I have actually put the miles in to find them. This province rewards those who are willing to embrace a little grit and leave the guidebook behind. Tell me what kind of untamed adventure you are looking for, and we will hit the dirt to uncover everything Imereti has to offer.
