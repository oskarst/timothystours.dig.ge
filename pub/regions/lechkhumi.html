Lechkhumi

Chestnut Woods & Sheer Cliffs

Lechkhumi is the smallest of the Georgian regions, so obscure that even many locals forget it exists. This means travelers can completely avoid the sanitized tourism industry and step into an untouched, rugged paradise. Enclosed by towering mountains on all sides, this territory is a labyrinth of sheer cliffs and chestnut forests that project a profound, raw reality.

Khvamli Limestone Caves

At the center of it all rises Khvamli, a massive limestone cliff wrapped in ancient mysteries and millennia-old mythology. Stepping off the paved routes leads to hidden caves carved with astrological symbols and sweeping views of the Black Sea and Mount Elbrus. This is not a polished museum exhibit; it is a visceral encounter with history earned through muddy boots.

Akhalchala Ridge Isolation

For those who crave real adventure, the steep and forbidding Lechkhumi Ridge offers narrow, trackless trails and desolate beauty. The journey up to Akhalchala reveals a rustic summer village set in a large meadow surrounded by the region's highest peaks. Up here, the pure air and clear mountain streams provide the majestic isolation found only in the truly untamed corners of the world.

Obscure Local Vineyards

Lechkhumi also happens to grow some of the rarest and most delicious grapes in the country, carefully cultivated in small village vineyards. Instead of attending a corporate wine tasting, we share unpretentious, unforgettable vintages with local families who drink far better than anyone in a fancy boutique. I have explored these obscure pathways for years, and I am ready to guide you straight into the hidden secrets of Lechkhumi.
