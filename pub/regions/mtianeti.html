Mtianeti

Fierce Glacial Torrents

Mtianeti is a vast, forbidding land of fierce rivers and massive peaks that loom overhead and distort your sense of scale. Sanitized comforts do not exist out here; the grip of winter holds these valleys for more than half the year. Traveling into this territory means entering a rugged world where the terrain dictates the terms and ancient mountaineer traditions still hold strong.

Khevsureti & Tusheti

This is not a place for casual sightseeing from the window of an air-conditioned bus. The steep trails lead up to places like Khevsureti and Tusheti, where ancient defensive towers spring from the green ridges. The mountaineers who live here are sober, serious folk who maintain stone shrines where old pagan customs flow together with Christian traditions in the shadow of the peaks.

Datvisjvari & Gudamakari

When the brief summer arrives and the high pastures bloom with rhododendrons, the intense isolation sets the mind free. Trekkers can go for days out of the sight of civilization, sleeping in tents or sharing rustic shelter with local herders. Whether crossing the Datvisjvari Pass or fording the rushing rivers of Gudamakari, every step must be earned.

Unyielding Off-Road Terrain

I have spent years exploring these endless, fascinating valleys, and I still feel like there is always another secret waiting over the next ridge. My job is to handle the off-road driving and the tough logistics so you can fully immerse yourself in the raw reality of the mountains. Grab your boots, and we will discover the true, unyielding character of Mtianeti.
