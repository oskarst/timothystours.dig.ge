Meskheti

Desolate Mtkvari Gorges

Meskheti is a stark, dramatic region defined by the mighty Mtkvari canyon and the heavy, lingering presence of the Middle Ages. Most tourists hurry out and back on sterile day trips just to snap a photo of the Vardzia cave town before rushing back to the city. We bypass that artificial rush, leaving the crowds behind to explore the massive, forgotten fortresses and ancient tunnels scattered across the desolate plateaus.

Tmogvi Castle Grapevines

This is a place where the history is as rugged as the terrain itself. Hiking up to the ruins of Tmogvi Castle reveals the tangled roots of a 400-year-old grapevine hidden deep in the valley below. There are no velvet ropes here; visitors experience the visceral reality of medieval architecture slowly being reclaimed by the untamed environment.

Tori Peaks & Pagan Idols

Beyond the canyon, the misty ridges of the Tori mountains offer an intense, isolated hiking experience free from other travelers. The route tracks through forgotten forests to find unmarked hot springs and the alien-like Stone Man of Uraveli, one of the last surviving pagan idols in the country. This is real exploration, where every discovery feels earned by the sweat on your brow.

Open Steppes & Local Noodles

I have driven these open steppes and walked these rocky paths to uncover the true character of Meskheti. Traveling with me means eating unique local noodles in authentic village guesthouses and sleeping under the vast, open sky. Grab your boots, and we will head out to find the ancient mysteries that this area holds.
