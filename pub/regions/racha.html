Racha

Wild Rioni Rapids

Racha shares the spectacular Rioni river valley with its twin region, Lechkhumi, offering a deeply authentic alternative to crowded tourist hotspots. There are no sanitized package tours here. Instead, the river flows wild and swift near its birthplace in the high mountains, setting the unhurried rhythm of village life.

Nikortsminda Carvings

While others rush from one famous landmark to the next, we take the time to explore the quiet grandeur of Racha's historical sites. Standing before the intricate stone carvings of Nikortsminda and the isolated beauty of Mravaldzali brings you face to face with the Great Caucasus. These are places of deep, grounded history appreciated best far from the busy highways.

Udziro Alpine Camp

The high mountains of Upper Racha are an absolute paradise for those willing to put in the physical effort. The trail passes through exquisite pine forests and forgotten wilderness before reaching camp by the pristine, isolated waters of Udziro Lake. This environment demands respect and repays the effort with unfiltered panoramas of the peaks.

Karstic Grottos & Waterfalls

I have spent some of my happiest days wandering these valleys, learning the hidden secrets of the karstic caves and towering waterfalls. My job is to handle the logistics so you can simply focus on the dirt under your feet and the incredible scale of the mountains above. Jump in the off-road car, and we will find the cozy, untamed adventures waiting in Racha.
