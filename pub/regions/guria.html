Guria

Lesser Caucasus Foothills

Most tourists just drive straight through Guria on their way to crowded seacoast resorts, missing out on one of the most charming parts of Georgia. Their loss is your gain. Nestled in the foothills of the Lesser Caucasus, this small, cozy area offers a genuine connection to the local culture. We bypass the sterile tourist traps and step directly into a world of sharp humor and incredibly warm hospitality.

Shemokmedi & Jikheti

The subtropical climate here is perfect for growing mandarins and unique varieties of grapes. Instead of rushing through a scripted itinerary, we take the time to walk or cycle through peaceful villages and explore small medieval churches like Shemokmedi and Jikheti. When the heat rises, local river swimming holes offer a perfect escape.

Raw Wildflower Herding

For those willing to embrace a little grit, the forested foothills and high mountains to the south offer a completely different reality. Leaving the overbuilt resorts behind, the route ventures into the rolling green peaks where summer herding families manufacture cheese amidst endless wildflower fields. This is the raw, unpolished side of the country that you came to experience.

Blueberry Leaf Tea Trails

Guria also produces some of the finest tea west of China, including a fermented mountain blueberry leaf tea that leaves a lasting impression. I have put the miles in to find these authentic experiences so you do not have to guess where the real Georgia hides. Grab your boots, and we will uncover the rugged, off-road beauty of Guria together.
