The Full Adventure

$240 / day

Includes a private guide, driver, and translator. You will hit the dirt roads together, exploring the unseen sights and rugged character of Georgia.

Mountain & Off-Road

$300 / day

Specialized trips to remote mountain regions (Kazbegi, Svaneti, Khevsureti, Tusheti, Upper Adjara, Gudamakari, Vashlovani) or rugged off-road routes.

These rates apply for groups of up to four adults (the size of the Highlander: can include two children as well). Larger groups are of course welcome, but require hiring a larger vehicle; contact me for a quote applicable to your specific needs. On trips with overnight stays outside of Tbilisi, guests are asked to cover my overnight expenses (either at the same accommodation or with a per diem for my own accommodation). Rates exclude third-party activity fees, meals, entry tickets, and planning fees. If you travel in the low season (November to April), I may be able to offer a discount (contact me for a specific quote).
